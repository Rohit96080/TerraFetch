Sentinel Loader

Version 0.2  ·  Search, preview, load and export Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery for the current map extent.

Contents: Quick start · Satellites & products · Forest fire · Area of interest · Preview, Load, Export · Layer names · Limitations · Troubleshooting · Version history

1. Quick start

  1. Zoom the QGIS map canvas to your area. The visible canvas extent is the search area.
  2. Choose Satellite, Collection (one collection, or All collections) and Product.
  3. Set the From / To dates and, for optical sensors, the maximum cloud cover.
  4. Click Search scenes. Up to 100 of the newest scenes are listed.
  5. Select a scene. Click Preview for a quick look, Load in QGIS to add it as a layer, or Export GeoTIFF to save a local copy.

Imagery comes from the free Microsoft Planetary Computer. No account is needed, but an internet connection is.

All collections searches every collection of the chosen satellite and merges the results into one list, newest first. A product is usable only for scenes whose collection supports it. Radar has no cloud cover, so the cloud filter is hidden for Sentinel-1.

Each scene row shows: date · cloud % (or orbit direction for radar) · platform · scene ID.

3. Forest fire (QuickFire)

Sentinel-2 only. Active-fire hotspots are detected from the shortwave-infrared bands (B11 + B12) and tinted over a natural-colour image. Based on QuickFire v1.0.0 by Pierre Markuse (CC BY 4.0).

ControlEffect
Fire sensitivity (0.5 – 2.0)Higher values mark more pixels as hotspots
Hotspot styleHeat tint, solid red, solid yellow, or subtle red shift
Show burn scarsBrightens dark, recently burned ground

The result is computed on your computer and saved as a temporary GeoTIFF, so it is slower than the streamed products. Clouds are masked with the scene classification band. Results are indicative only and must not be used for operational fire detection.

4. Area of interest (AOI) and size limits

The AOI is the map canvas extent at the moment you click Search, converted to longitude/latitude and clipped to the world (±180°, ±90°). If you move or zoom the map afterwards, search again.

ActionMaximum AOIApprox. size
Search, Preview, Load (streamed products)No limit-
Export GeoTIFF1.5° wide and highabout 165 km
Forest fire (Preview excluded)0.5° wide and highabout 55 km

If the AOI is too large the tool shows a message. Zoom in, search again, then retry. A scene may cover only part of your AOI; areas outside the scene are empty or transparent. If the AOI does not touch the scene at all, Preview and Export fail.

If a streamed layer stops drawing after about an hour, remove it and load the scene again. Composite bands are raw reflectance: adjust the stretch in Layer Styling. Radar is linear backscatter power.

6. Layer names

Layers are named <scene id>_<product>, with spaces as underscores. For Sentinel-2, for example:
S2C_MSIL2A_20261017T003701_True_colour_(RGB)
Sentinel-1 and Landsat use the full scene ID.

7. Limitations

  • Source: only Planetary Computer collections are offered. Not available: Sentinel-2 L1C, Sentinel-1 GRD/SLC, Sentinel-3, Sentinel-5P, and Landsat Level-1 for Landsat 4-9 (they would need Copernicus Data Space or USGS accounts).
  • Search: maximum 100 scenes per collection, no paging. The cloud filter is applied after that cap.
  • One scene at a time: no mosaicking of neighbouring tiles.
  • AOI: fixed at search time; export up to 1.5°, fire product up to 0.5° (see section 4).
  • Streaming: links expire after about 1 hour. Remote reads do not use the QGIS proxy settings.
  • Preview: auto-stretched, so colours differ from QGIS. For the fire product it runs the full calculation at low resolution.
  • Fire product: 20 m bands are resampled to 10 m (blocky hotspots); bright soil, sun glint or hot industrial sites can cause false alarms and small fires can be missed; Sentinel-2 revisits about every 5 days, so it is not real time.
  • Landsat MSS: band names are untested.
  • Housekeeping: temporary files are not deleted automatically. No automated tests yet.
